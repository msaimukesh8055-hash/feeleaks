# Public fee cases — research for owner review

Collected Oct 5, 2026. **Not live on the site.** Nothing here is published until the owner approves each entry.

## What this is

Fee complaints and official actions about named Indian educational institutions, gathered from news reports, official orders (fee committees, district collectors, courts, consumer commissions, child-rights bodies) and forums. Each entry gives only what the source says, with a link and date. Where a school responded, its response is included.

**How to read entries**

- **Source type:** `Official` = a regulator, court, collector or commission acted (strongest). `News` = a newspaper reported parents' allegations. `Forum` = an individual's post (weakest; never published without corroboration).
- **Checked:** `Read` = the article itself was read and the figures copied from it. `Summary` = only a search summary was available (article blocked); needs checking before use.
- Words like "parents say" and "the order says" matter. These are **allegations or findings reported by the source**, not FeeLeaks' own conclusions.
- No names of children, parents or complainants are copied, even where the source printed them.

## Numbers and limits

- **36 cases covering 53 named institutions** (schools, colleges, coaching centres) in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Hyderabad, Chennai, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Himachal and J&K.
- This is short of the 100 we hoped for. Two reasons:
  - **Reddit blocks automated access** (both direct requests and the fetch tool), so Reddit posts are included only where a news article reported on them, and most such articles don't name the school.
  - **Times of India, Hindustan Times, Indian Express, The Hindu, NDTV, New Indian Express, The Telegraph and Bangalore Mirror block the research tools.** Most named-school stories in Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Jaipur and Lucknow are in those papers. Coverage of those cities is therefore thin.
- Many articles describe city-wide hikes without naming a school. Those are listed under "Context" and are not counted.
- Older cases (before 2021) were skipped unless they were official actions.

## Context — no institution named (useful for dashboards and rules, not as cases)

- **National:** LocalCircles 2026 survey of 47,000+ parents in 271 districts: 70% saw hikes of 30%+ over three years. [LocalCircles](https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/school-fee-increase-survey-2026)
- **Delhi:** 600 schools inspected (Apr 2025), 10+ show-cause notices; later 970 inspected, 150+ notices. Delhi School Education (Transparency in Fixation and Regulation of Fees) Act, 2025 froze fees at 1 Apr 2025 levels. Delhi HC (Mar 2026) deferred school-level fee committees. A Saket school allegedly raised fees 50–80% and withheld roll numbers. NCPCR flagged an unnamed Vasant Kunj school (Mar 2025).
- **Karnataka:** child rights commission received 300+ complaints in 2025 (hikes up to 40%, forced vendor purchases, full-year fee demanded before transfer certificates); notices to three schools of a "prestigious group" in Central Bengaluru, not named. [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/schools-abnormal-fee-hike-parents-complain-to-child-rights-panel-3501422). Top Bengaluru engineering college management seat ₹64 lakh (from ₹54 lakh), not named.
- **Telangana:** unnamed Hyderabad schools reported charging a ₹45,000 "initiation fee". Two unnamed engineering colleges (Ghatkesar, Nadurgul) collecting ₹27,000–47,000 over the prescribed fee; fee regulator's 28 Jul 2026 circular calls excess "capitation". Fee regulator: MBBS tuition only for 4.5 years.
- **Madhya Pradesh:** Jabalpur — 5 further schools told to refund ₹31.51 crore to 52,480 students (Nov 2024). Narmadapuram — 79 schools found with 10–20% hikes (2024). Schools list 6 fee heads on the state portal but charge 20–25.
- **Punjab:** 5% annual cap (2026); refund of hikes above the cap for past three years; no withholding of exam cards; no forced vendors.
- **Uttarakhand:** Dehradun DM probed 25 schools (Apr 2025), found hikes up to 35%; cap of 10%; free choice of book and uniform shops. (St Joseph's Academy and Summer Valley School were named only as among those examined — no finding attributed to them.)
- **Rajasthan:** private medical colleges told to refund unapproved "management quota" fees with 12% interest (Oct 2025), not named.
- **Bihar (Patna):** 15–30% hikes; private book sets ₹6,000–8,000 vs ₹1,000–1,300 for NCERT (Classes 9–10).
- **Kerala:** Thiruvananthapuram consumer commission ordered an unnamed NEET coaching centre to refund ₹70,600 of ₹82,600 (Aug 2026); "no refund" clauses held unfair.
